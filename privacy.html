Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

17 Jewels ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Email address and password when you create an account
  • Watch Data: Photos and details of pocket watches you add to your collection
  • Profile Information: Optional profile photo you may upload

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process and store your watch collection data
  • Send you technical notices and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions

AI-Powered Features

Our app uses artificial intelligence to analyze photos of your pocket watches and identify maker, model, year, and other specifications. Photos are processed securely and are only used to provide you with identification results. We do not use your photos to train AI models or share them with third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud infrastructure provider. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your watch collection is private and only visible to you unless you explicitly choose to share it using our sharing features.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Delete your account at any time from within the app

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, all your data including watch photos and collection information will be permanently deleted from our servers.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
